# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Expected behavior includes constructive technical discussion, respect for differing opinions, and accepting good-faith correction. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, threats, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers using the contact channel documented by the project owner. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action they consider appropriate.
